**Rule ID:** MET-007

**Description:** Metrics do not carry volatile or descriptive resource attributes.

**Rationale:** Many metric backends treat resource attributes as part of a time series' identity (its label set). Some resource attributes are volatile or verbose:

* `host.ip` and `host.mac` list every network interface on the host, and change as interfaces come and go.
* `process.command_args` and `process.command_line` often include values that differ between restarts, such as ports, paths, or timestamps.
* `process.runtime.description` and `os.description` are free-form strings, redundant with `process.runtime.name`, `process.runtime.version`, `os.type`, and `os.version`.

When these values change, every metric of the service starts a new series, breaking continuity in dashboards and alerts. Even when stable, they add overhead to every metric export without being useful for aggregation. They may still be valuable on spans and logs; for metrics, host and process metadata is better conveyed through entities.

**Target:** Metric

**Criteria:** Resource attributes of metric data MUST NOT include any of the following keys: `host.ip`, `host.mac`, `process.command_args`, `process.command_line`, `process.runtime.description`, `os.description`.

**Impact:** Normal
